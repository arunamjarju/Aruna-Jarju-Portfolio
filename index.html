ABOUT

Research, technology, and education with practical impact.

My work brings together computing, environmental sustainability, data analysis, and education. I focus on developing data-driven and computational solutions for sustainability, energy efficiency, environmental analysis, telecommunications, intelligent systems, and evidence-based decision making.

I am particularly interested in research that can be translated into practical tools, stronger systems, training, and institutional outcomes.
